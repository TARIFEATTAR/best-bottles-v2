Contract Packaging

Do you need packaging services for small quantities?

Not only do we sell glass bottles, we also offer contract packaging services for filling, capping, labeling and packaging the perfume bottles sold by us. We specialize in small runs of hundreds to a few thousand bottles.
Our facilities are designed for filling perfumes and fragrant materials. We are not approved for filling or handling any pharmaceuticals.

Filling of small vials, Roll on bottles and spray bottles.

5ml bottles filled with perfume oils.

We have the capability to fill small perfume vials with capacities of 1ml to bottles with capacities of  up to 150ml.

Customers can select bottles from our large assortment of bottles. In addition to bottles, we also have a large selection of fragrances and essential oils. More information about our high quality fragrances can be obtained from our website, www.NematFragrances.com and www.NematInternational.com. Customers can provide their own perfumes or essential oils for filling and packaging.

Capping and Plugging.

Small 1ml perfume sample vials are filled and plugged in specialized trays. Torque controlled cappers are used for screwing caps onto small bottles. Caps on larger bottles are applied by hand. Roll on bottles are plugged with a roll on plug and then capped.

5ml bottles filled with perfume oils and capped.
1ml vials filled with perfume oils and plugged

Labeling , Screen printing and Laser engraving.


Labels applied onto small 5ml rectangular bottles.

Labels can be applied onto small perfume bottles. Labels on small rectangular bottles labels are applied by hand, whereas cylindrical bottles are labeled by labeling machines. Labels have to be supplied in roll form.


Engraved metal shell atomizers

In addition to labeling, screen printing can be done on bottles. Screen printing can be done on very small, for example 1ml vials, to large bottles. Screen printing is generally limited to one color and requires artwork, a screen to be made and set up to do the printing. After printing the ink on the bottle, the bottle is heated to create a strong bond between the ink and glass.

Laser engraving can be done on aluminum containers such as metal shell perfume atomizer. It is best to use dark colored atomizers, such as Black, Blue or Red, so that the engraving can be seen clearly.

Packing

Filled and labeled products can be packaged in gift boxes provided by the customer. Individual boxes are packed into a larger carton for shipment to customer. We provide velveteen bags, organza bags and some gift boxes. Customers can provide their own packaging boxes.

Pricing

Pricing depends on a lot of factors, such as

  1. Quantity of bottles needed to be filled.
  2. Type of liquid to be filled and quantity of liquid to be filled in each bottle.
  3. Type of bottle to be labeled – cylindrical or flat surface.
  4. Type of closure needed for securing the liquid – Roll on plugs, spray tops, droppers, etc.
  5. Packing of individual bottles in boxes, bags, etc.

A quote will be provided based on the complexity of the project and estimated time required.
