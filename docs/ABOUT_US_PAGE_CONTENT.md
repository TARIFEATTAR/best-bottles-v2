## OUR STORY

### The Beginning
Best Bottles was founded in 1995 in Union City, California, with a simple mission: to provide small-batch makers with access to the same quality packaging used by major fragrance houses—without the impossible minimums.

What started as a small warehouse serving local perfumers has grown into a nationwide supplier, trusted by thousands of makers across the United States and around the world.

### What We Believe
We believe that great products deserve great packaging. Your formula is the result of countless hours of refinement. The vessel that holds it should reflect that same dedication to craft.

That's why we obsess over the details others overlook: the precision of a neck finish, the clarity of the glass, the satisfying click of a well-fitted cap.

### 🔬 Technical Precision
Every bottle, cap, and fitment is specified to industry standards. When we say "18-415 finish," we mean it—with tolerances measured in fractions of a millimeter. No leaks. No surprises.

Need 12 bottles to test a new line? We've got you. Ready to scale to 1,000? Same quality, better pricing. We serve makers at every stage of growth.

### 🎓 Expert Guidance
