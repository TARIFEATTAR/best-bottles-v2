# Brand Guidelines: Best Bottles (2026 Redesign)

## 1. Brand Strategy
**Mission:** best Bottles exists to elevate the craft of artisan perfumers and skincare makers by providing premium, reliable, and beautiful packaging solutions.
**Target Audience:**
*   **The Maker (Etsy/Craft/Market):** Needs small quantities, high style, and guidance.
*   **The Small Business (Boutique/Spa):** Needs consistency, reliability, and wholesale pricing.
*   **The Middle Market:** Scaling brands that need a polished, professional look.

**Core Values:**
*   **Elegance:** Beauty in simplicity.
*   **Reliability:** Technical precision (no leaks, correct neck finishes).
*   **Empowerment:** Helping our customers build *their* brands.

---

## 2. Visual Identity: "Muted Luxury"
The new "Best Bottles" aesthetic shifts away from the generic "warehouse supplier" look to a curated, boutique atelier vibe. It feels expensive but approachable—like a high-end architectural firm or a luxury stationery brand.

### A. Color Palette
The colors are inspired by nature (botanicals) and industry (glass, metal). They are soft, desaturated, and harmonious.

| Color Name | Hex Code | Description | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sage Glass** | `#8FA395` | A muted, dusty green. Represents glass, nature, and essential oils. | Primary Accent (Buttons, Headers) |
| **Charcoal** | `#2E3133` | A soft, matte dark grey. NOT pure black (`#000000`). | Primary Text, Logos, Borders |
| **Champagne** | `#D4C5A9` | A subtle metallic gold/beige. Represents luxury and oil essence. | Highlights, Icons, secondary buttons |

| **Slate Hint** | `#607d8b` | A blue-grey undertone. | Secondary text, borders |

### B. Typography
We mix a character-rich Serif for headings with a clean, technical Sans-Serif for product data.

**Primary Typeface (Headings/Brand):** *EB Garamond*
*   Why: It strikes the perfect balance—classic and legible, widely available (Google Fonts), but with a humanist touch that feels "savvy" and "smart" rather than haughty or overly fashion-focused.
*   Usage: H1, H2, Hero Banners.

**Secondary Typeface (Body/Specs):** *Inter* or *Lato*
*   Why: Extremely legible at small sizes. Critical for reading "15ml" or "20-400" spec charts clearly.
*   Usage: Product descriptions, pricing, navigation, specs.

### C. Logo Concepts
We have developed directions for the logo redesign based on the "Smart Sophistication" approach:
1.  **The "Savvy Wordmark" (Chosen Direction):** Using **EB Garamond** for a clean, timeless look. It avoids the "crafty" Etsy look but isn't as imposing as a luxury fashion house. It says "We know bottles."
2.  **The "Minimalist Line":** A single elegant line drawing of a bottle with a drop.
3.  **The "Abstract":** A geometric representation of glass and nature.

## 3. Voice & Tone
Our voice is **The Expert Atelier**. We are not just a warehouse; we are curators.

*   **Do:** Be helpful, precise, and encouraging. Use words like "Curated," "Collection," "Finish," "Essence."
*   **Don't:** Be overly salesy, "cheap," or robotic. Avoid "Buy Now!!", "Cheap Bottles", "Huge Blowout."

**Example Rewrite:**
*   *Old:* "Cheap 10ml roll on bottles for sale. Buy bulk."
*   *New:* "The 10ml Classic Roll-On Collection. Premium glass with leak-proof fitments. Available in bulk tiers."

---

## 4. UI/UX Elements
*   **Buttons:** Flat, sharp corners or slightly rounded (2px). Ghost buttons for secondary actions.
*   **Photography:** High-contrast, well-lit studio shots. No busy backgrounds. Show the bottle "in action" (e.g., next to a botanical ingredient).
*   **Whitespace:** Use generous padding. Let the products breathe.
