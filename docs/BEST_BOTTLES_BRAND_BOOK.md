# SLIDE 3: THE LOGO – Primary Usage

## Primary Logo
The Best Bottles wordmark uses **EB Garamond** in its Regular weight. This classic serif typeface communicates expertise, trust, and timeless quality without feeling overly luxurious or inaccessible.

### Primary Logo Specifications
- **Font:** EB Garamond Regular
- **Color:** Charcoal (#2E3133)
- **Alignment:** Centered or Left-aligned
- **Minimum Size:** 120px wide for digital, 1.5" for print

### Logo Variations

| Version | Use Case | Background |
|---------|----------|------------|
| **Primary (Dark)** | Standard use | Light/White backgrounds |
| **Reversed (Light)** | Dark backgrounds | Dark/Photography overlays |
| **Monochrome Black** | Print, Fax, Legal | Any |
| **Monochrome White** | Dark surfaces | Photography, banners |

### Clear Space
Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "B" in "Best" on all sides. No text, images, or graphic elements should enter this zone.

# SLIDE 4: LOGO DON'TS

## Logo Misuse Examples
Protect the integrity of the Best Bottles brand by avoiding these common mistakes:

### ❌ DO NOT: Stretch or Distort
Never alter the proportions of the logo. Always scale proportionally.

### ❌ DO NOT: Change the Colors
Do not apply unapproved colors, gradients, or effects to the logo.

### ❌ DO NOT: Add Effects
No drop shadows, glows, bevels, or 3D effects.

### ❌ DO NOT: Rotate
The logo should always appear level and horizontal.

### ❌ DO NOT: Place on Busy Backgrounds
Avoid placing the logo on complex photography or patterns without a solid backing shape.

### ❌ DO NOT: Alter the Typography
Never retype the logo in a different font or modify letter spacing.

### ❌ DO NOT: Crowd the Logo
Always maintain the required clear space around the logo.

### ❌ DO NOT: Use Low Resolution
Always use vector (SVG/EPS) or high-resolution PNG files.

- **HEX:** #F9F8F4
- **RGB:** 249, 248, 244
- **CMYK:** 0, 0, 2, 2

- **HEX:** #607D8B

All text combinations have been tested for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance:
- ✅ Charcoal on Paper White: Contrast Ratio 12.5:1 (Passes AAA)
- ✅ Charcoal on Champagne: Contrast Ratio 7.2:1 (Passes AA)
- ✅ Paper White on Sage Glass: Contrast Ratio 4.8:1 (Passes AA for large text)

# SLIDE 6: TYPOGRAPHY

## Primary Typeface: EB Garamond
**Google Fonts Link:** https://fonts.google.com/specimen/EB+Garamond

EB Garamond is a revival of Claude Garamont's 16th-century typeface. It is elegant, highly readable, and carries a sense of heritage and expertise—perfect for a brand that has been in business since 1995.

### Usage
- **Headlines (H1):** EB Garamond Medium, 48px
- **Subheadlines (H2):** EB Garamond Regular, 32px
- **Section Headers (H3):** EB Garamond Regular, 24px
- **Pull Quotes:** EB Garamond Italic, 20px

### Example
# The Art of Packaging
## Premium Glass for Discerning Makers

---

## Secondary Typeface: Inter
**Google Fonts Link:** https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Inter is a modern, highly legible sans-serif designed specifically for screen readability. Its clean lines and open apertures make it ideal for product specifications, pricing, and UI elements.

### Usage
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular, 16px, Line Height 1.6
- **Product Specs:** Inter Medium, 14px
- **Captions/Metadata:** Inter Regular, 12px
- **Buttons:** Inter SemiBold, 14px, ALL CAPS, Letter Spacing +1px
- **Navigation:** Inter Medium, 14px

### Example
> Our 10ml Cobalt Blue Roll-On Bottles feature precision-machined necks (18-415 finish) and are compatible with both plastic and stainless steel roller balls. Available in cases of 144 units.

# SLIDE 7: IMAGERY STYLE

## Photography Direction

### Product Photography
- **Background:** Clean, neutral (Paper White #F9F8F4 or soft grey gradient)
- **Lighting:** High-key, even lighting with subtle reflections to show glass clarity
- **Angle:** 3/4 front view as primary; additional angles for detail
- **Props:** Minimal. When used, incorporate natural botanicals (lavender, eucalyptus, dried citrus) to suggest essential oils

### Lifestyle/Context Photography
- **Setting:** Clean workspaces, apothecary-style shelving, maker studios
- **Tone:** Warm, natural light. Muted, desaturated color grading.
- **Subject:** Focus on the craft—hands filling bottles, arranging products, applying labels
- **Avoid:** Overly staged "stock photo" looks, harsh flash, cluttered backgrounds

### Image Treatment
- **Saturation:** Slightly reduced (−10% to −20%)
- **Contrast:** Medium-high for clarity
- **Color Temperature:** Warm (slight shift towards Paper White tones)

# SLIDE 8: UI ELEMENTS

## Buttons

### Primary Button
- **Background:** Sage Glass (#8FA395)
- **Text:** Paper White (#F9F8F4)
- **Font:** Inter SemiBold, 14px, ALL CAPS, +1px tracking
- **Padding:** 16px vertical, 32px horizontal
- **Border Radius:** 4px (subtle rounding)
- **Hover State:** Darken background by 10%

### Secondary Button (Ghost)
- **Background:** Transparent
- **Border:** 1px solid Charcoal (#2E3133)
- **Text:** Charcoal (#2E3133)
- **Hover State:** Background fills with Charcoal, text becomes Paper White

### Text Link
- **Color:** Sage Glass (#8FA395)
- **Style:** Underline on hover only
- **Font:** Inter Regular, inherit size

## Dividers & Borders
- **Color:** Slate Hint (#607D8B) at 30% opacity
- **Weight:** 1px hairline

## Cards & Containers
- **Background:** Pure White (#FFFFFF) or Paper White (#F9F8F4)
- **Border:** 1px solid Slate Hint at 20% opacity
- **Shadow:** 0px 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06) — very subtle
- **Border Radius:** 8px

# SLIDE 9: VOICE & TONE

## Brand Voice: "The Expert Atelier"

We speak with the confidence of 30 years of experience, but we remain approachable and helpful. We are educators, not salespeople.

### We ARE:
- **Knowledgeable:** "The 18-415 finish ensures a secure seal for oils up to moderate viscosity."
- **Helpful:** "Not sure which size? Our 5ml is perfect for sampling; our 30ml is ideal for retail."
- **Precise:** "Capacity: 10ml ±0.5ml. Neck Finish: 18-415. Height: 62mm."
- **Encouraging:** "You've got the formula. We've got the vessel. Let's make something beautiful."

### We are NOT:
- **Salesy:** ~~"BUY NOW! HUGE SALE! LOWEST PRICES!"~~
- **Vague:** ~~"Good quality bottles for cheap."~~
- **Condescending:** ~~"As you probably don't know..."~~
- **Overly Casual:** ~~"Hey! These bottles are super cute lol"~~

### Example Copy Rewrites

| ❌ Before | ✅ After |
|-----------|----------|
| "Cheap roll-on bottles for sale" | "The Classic Roll-On Collection: Precision-fit glass at wholesale value" |
| "Buy more, save more!" | "Volume pricing available for orders of 144+ units" |
| "Click here" | "Explore the Collection" or "View Specifications" |
