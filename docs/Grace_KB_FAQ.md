## 1. Product & Usage Questions (The "Bottle Expert" Section)

### **Essentials vs. Perfumes: What bottle do I need?**

**Q: I am making essential oil blends. Which bottles are compatible?**
A: For essential oils, always use **glass bottles**.
*   **Best Choice:** Amber or Cobalt Blue glass bottles are recommended because they protect the oils from UV light degradation.
*   **Clear Glass:** Safe to use but keep out of direct sunlight.
*   **Plastic:** **Avoid plastic** for pure essential oils as undiluted oils can degrade plastic over time.

**Q: I am making perfume sprays (alcohol-based). What can I use?**
A: You can use both glass and aluminum bottles.
*   **Glass:** The industry standard for premium perfumes.
*   **Aluminum:** Great for durability and travel-size sprays.
*   **Plastic:** Only use PET plastic for dilute alcohol mixtures, but extended testing is recommended.

**Q: What is the difference between a "Roll-On" and a "Spray"?**
A:
*   **Roll-On:** Uses a roller ball (plastic or metal) to glide oil onto the skin. Best for perfume oils, aromatherapy blends, and lip oils.
*   **Spray (Atomizer):** Mists the liquid. Best for alcohol-based perfumes, body mists, and room sprays. **Do not use sprayers for thick oils** as they will clog the nozzle.

**Q: Why do some roll-ons leak?**
A: Leaking usually occurs if the "housing" (plug) isn't fully seated or if the oil is too thin for the ball tolerance. We offer **Fitment Testing** to ensure our glass and fitments match perfectly. Ensure you snap the roller ball housing *all the way down* until it sits flush with the bottle neck.

**Q: What are "Neck Finishes" like 20-400 or 18-415?**
A: These numbers tell you what cap fits what bottle.
*   **First number (e.g., 20):** The diameter of the neck in millimeters.
*   **Second number (e.g., 400):** The thread style (how many times it twists significantly).
*   **Rule of Thumb:** A 20-400 cap ONLY fits a 20-400 bottle. You cannot mix and match sizes.

### **Common Bottle Sizes & Applications**
*   **1ml - 3ml:** Samples, testers, "drams".
*   **5ml - 10ml:** Travel sizes, roller-ball perfumes, lip gloss.
*   **15ml - 30ml (0.5oz - 1oz):** Standard retail perfume sizes, beard oils, serums.
*   **50ml - 100ml (1.7oz - 3.4oz):** Large eau de parfum bottles, room sprays, body oils.
